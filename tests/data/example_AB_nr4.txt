AB  - This pilot study investigates whether EEG-based neurofeedback improves attention regulation in children diagnosed with ADHD. We conducted a 10-week intervention with 32 participants and observed moderate improvements in sustained attention scores compared to baseline. Limitations include the small sample size and the need for longer-term follow-up.

AB  - We compare the feasibility and preliminary effectiveness of cognitive-behavioral therapy versus neurofeedback within public schools. In a quasi-experimental design (n=68), both approaches showed benefits; however, neurofeedback required specialized staff and equipment. Cost and implementation barriers are discussed.

AB  - This feasibility trial explores real-time EEG feedback to support classroom engagement. Twenty adolescents participated over four weeks. Engagement proxies improved during feedback sessions; however, transfer to non-feedback contexts was inconsistent. Future work will investigate personalization and teacher training.
